FreeRTOS in the boot firmware of this IronFlockOS image

This image and its RAUC update bundle carry NVIDIA's boot firmware for Jetson
Orin modules, unchanged, as UEFI capsules from NVIDIA's package
nvidia-l4t-bootloader @L4T_PACKAGE_VERSION@ (Jetson Linux @L4T_VERSION@), in
/opt/ota_package/t23x/. The firmware of the Safety Processing Engine (SPE) in
